How it works

One house. One prompt. Nine clips.

The crew

12 people, one roof, 38 hours

Comedians, editors and filmers move into one house together for 38 hours and make comedy as a team.

The prompt

It starts with a line

Each round opens with an absurd, irresistible prompt. The team turns it into a minimum of 9 clips, each 60 seconds or less.

The contest

9 months, monthly winners

Each month one clip team wins a minimum of $300, plus whatever anyone else chips in: hotels, books, plane tickets, sessions, clothing, phones, tripods and more. Winners are announced at results events with our partner Humankind.

The house

Twin Pines: every room is a set

Our home base is a luxury mountain cabin in Munds Park, up in the pines north of Sedona. It sleeps 12 across 4 bedrooms and 9 beds. There's a great room with a stone fireplace, an emerald kitchen, a hot tub, a bunk room, and a covered patio. Every room is a ready-made set for a 60-second clip.
