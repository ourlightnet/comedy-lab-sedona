How it works

One house. 48 Hours. Viral Comedy.

The crew

12 people, one roof, 48 hours

Comedians, editors and filmers move into one house together for 48 hours and make comedy as a team.

The prompt

It starts with a line

Each round opens with an absurd, irresistible prompt. The team turns it into a 60-second-or-less micro clip for Instagram, TikTok and YouTube Shorts.

The reveal

Watch what happened

Then we show every clip live at Humankind.center, plus live improv. Friday, March 19, 2027, 8 to 10pm.

The house

Every Room is a Set

A cabin in the pines above Sedona where every room is a set: the emerald kitchen, the bunk room, the hot tub, all of it. Add a costume closet stuffed with ETs, angels and ascended masters, and anything can happen in 60 seconds. 👽👼✨

    The Experiment

    Year 1 is a prototype. Here's what we're doing right now.

    1. 🏡 One houseTwin Pines in Munds Park, above Sedona, March 7 to 10, 2027.
    2. 🎤 Nine comedians, two editorsOne roof, 48 hours, making comedy as a team.
    3. ✍️ Opening lines, picked by youYou vote on the lines that start the clips, and add your own.
    4. 🎬 60-second clipsShort, strange and made for Instagram, TikTok and YouTube Shorts.
    5. 🍿 The showing at HumankindEvery clip on the big screen, plus live improv. Friday, March 19, 2027, 8 to 10pm at Humankind.center.
    6. 📊 An open budgetFunded by the City of Sedona, LightNet and people like you, with every dollar accounted for.
