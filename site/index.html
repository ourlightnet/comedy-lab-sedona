How it works

One house. One prompt. Nine clips.

The crew

12 people under one roof

Comedians, editors and filmers move into one house together and make comedy as a team.

The prompt

It starts with a line

Each round opens with an absurd, irresistible prompt. The team turns it into a minimum of 9 clips, each 60 seconds or less.

The contest

9 months, monthly winners

Each month one clip team wins a minimum of $300, plus whatever anyone else chips in: hotels, books, plane tickets, sessions, clothing, phones, tripods and more. Winners are announced at results events with our partner Humankind.

The house

Twin Pines: our home base

A luxury mountain cabin in Munds Park, up in the pines north of Sedona. It sleeps 12 across 4 bedrooms and 9 beds, with 2.5 baths, a chef's kitchen, a hot tub, a bunk room and a fenced outdoor living area. Plenty of sets for 9 clips a round.
